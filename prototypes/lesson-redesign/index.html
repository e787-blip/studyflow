What this fixes, in all three

Found by going through every card type in lesson.html today.

  • The type is named once.Most cards say it three or four times: the badge, the blue heading, the chip, then an instruction line ("Fill in the blank" / "Complete the answer" / "Fill in the blank" / "Type the missing word").
  • The question is the biggest thing.The blue header above it was bigger and louder than the question it was for.
  • One Check button, always in the same place.There are five Check-button styles today, in four different places: inside the box, under it, after the feedback, or none.
  • Hint and Flag on every question.Seven types have no hint; only five have a flag.
  • You can see how far through you are.The nav showed the time left, but not how many questions were left.
  • The score is shown once.It was in the nav and again in every card's footer.
  • Dark mode everywhere.Many cards use light colours written straight into the card, with no dark version.
  • Every card has the same frame.The whiteboard cards had no card at all.

What stays the same

One blue (#4a7cf6) and the ink colour. No new accent colours.

Instrument Serif and DM Sans. The serif only ever at its one weight.

Green means right, red means wrong. Each answer gets a drawn tick or cross, so colour is never the only sign.

Amber only means "look here": hints, flags, "Not sure", the watch-out flashcard.

Pictures stay on paper in dark mode, so the drawings keep their lines.

The streak stays a small grey flame next to the score.

Keys: 1–4 pick an answer, Enter checks and continues, arrows turn the lesson and the flashcards.

Reduced motion turns every slide into a fade.

# StudyFlow logo kit

Mark: an **SF monogram** made of two cut-ribbon letters (S and F), each with 45-degree "paper cut" ends.
Colors: blue `#4a7cf6`, navy `#0e142a`, white. Letters are drawn as plain shapes, so there is no font licence for the mark.

## Files
- `src/symbol-sf.svg` master (black). `src/symbol-s-small.svg` is the **small-size cut**: the S alone, used for favicons and tiny icons.
- `svg/` black, white, blue, navy, square, favicon and app-icon versions (SVG + 512 px PNG), plus `studyflow-lockup-horizontal.svg`.
- `web/` favicon.ico, 16/32/48 px PNGs, apple-touch-icon, 192/512 and maskable icons, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html`.

## Rules
- Use the SF monogram at 40 px and larger. Below that, use the S alone.
- Clear space: keep at least the height of the S's top bar (about one fifth of the mark) free on all sides.
- One-colour: use black, white, or one of the two brand colors. No gradients or shadows.
- On a blue background use the white version. On white use blue or navy.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or separate the S from the F in the monogram.
- The lockup's "StudyFlow" is live text in Instrument Serif. Convert it to outlines before sending it to a printer.

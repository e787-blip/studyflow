Why we built StudyFlow

Every student knows the feeling — staring at a pile of notes before an exam, not knowing where to start. Most study tools just digitize the same old approach. StudyFlow is different. By combining AI with real cognitive science — spaced repetition, active recall, the Feynman technique — we built something that doesn't just organize your notes. It tells you exactly what to study, when, and in what order. Because students deserve better than guessing.

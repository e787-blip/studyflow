Meet the founder

Ethan Hwang

Founder & Creator — StudyFlow

Ethan is a student at Punahou School in Honolulu, Hawaii. Frustrated by the lack of genuinely effective study tools, he built StudyFlow from scratch — combining AI with real cognitive science research to create something that actually helps students learn, not just memorize. What started as a personal project became a fully working product used by students across Hawaii.

Why we built StudyFlow

Every student knows the feeling — staring at a pile of notes before an exam, not knowing where to start. Most study tools just digitize the same old approach.

StudyFlow is different. By combining AI with real cognitive science — spaced repetition, active recall, the Feynman technique — we built something that doesn't just organize your notes. It tells you exactly what to study, when, and in what order.

Because students deserve better than guessing.

Spaced repetition

Questions you get wrong come back automatically, right when each idea needs another look.

Active recall

You practice pulling answers from memory instead of rereading your notes.

The Feynman technique

Explain an idea in your own words and the AI tells you exactly what you missed.
